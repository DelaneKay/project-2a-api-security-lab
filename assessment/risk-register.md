# Project 2A — Security Risk Register

**Assessment date:** 2026-10-24  
**Target:** OWASP Juice Shop v20.3.0 training lab  
**Status convention:** Open — remediation not performed in this lab. Due dates are **TBD by service owner**. The service owner must decide whether to remediate, accept, or further assess each risk in a real service.

This register tracks the findings and security observations reconstructed from the assessment evidence. Ratings use the qualitative lab risk matrix in [assessment-report-inventory.md](assessment-report-inventory.md). NIST CSF mappings identify potentially relevant outcomes for organizational tracking; they do not mean the application is compliant or noncompliant with CSF. The detailed register for the 306 SAST and 87 SCA entries is in [risk-register-sca-sast.csv](risk-register-sca-sast.csv); it includes proposed owners, open/untriaged status, owner-set due dates, evidence references, residual-risk notes, and NIST mapping status for each record.

The detailed ZAP alert inventory, with separate generic baseline, AJAX baseline, direct Spider discovery, and active API Scan results, is in [dast-individual-findings.md](dast-individual-findings.md). Counts with example URLs remain grouped when the source output did not preserve all instances; repeated alerts across runs are cross-referenced rather than counted as new underlying risks.

The request-by-request manual API checks and findings are in [manual-api-findings.md](manual-api-findings.md), including which tests were manual-only and which overlapped ZAP coverage.

## Register maintenance

- **Owner:** The service owner assigns a named engineer or team; proposed teams above are defaults for this lab exercise.
- **Due date:** Set by the service owner using production exposure, data sensitivity, exploitability, and delivery constraints. No calendar dates are invented here.
- **Status:** Remains open until remediation is implemented and independently verified, or an authorized owner documents risk acceptance with rationale and review date.
- **Residual risk:** Record what remains after treatment or why the risk is accepted. Current entries describe the risk in the vulnerable lab and are not acceptance decisions.
- **Framework use:** NIST CSF outcome mappings help connect risk treatment to organizational outcomes. They are not proof of compliance and should be refined by the control owner.
- **Completeness:** Individual SCA/SAST records from the pasted output are present in the linked CSV. Add per-instance ZAP rows if a structured alert export is available. The six existing GitHub issues cover selected API risks, not the 393 individual Snyk entries.
